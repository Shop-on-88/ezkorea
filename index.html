    • Long nails?Press the release tab with the pad of your thumb, not your fingertip.
    • Protect the glassLay a folded towel on the windshield while the blade is off. A bare arm can snap back and chip the glass.
    • Do one side at a timeKeep the old blade next to you so you can match the direction of the new one.
    • Listen for the clickGive the blade a gentle tug afterwards. If it stays put, you're done.

      FAQ

      Flat D or Hybrid A: which should I buy?

      If your arm is a U-hook, both fit. Hybrid A costs less and has a sturdy covered frame. Flat D has a slimmer beam design and comes with adapters for other arm types, so pick it if you're not sure what your arm is, or if your car has a side pin or push button.

      Is a set one blade or two?

      A set is two blades for the front windshield: one driver side and one passenger side, each in the size your car needs.

      My car isn't on the list.

      Measure your old blades from end to end, or read the size printed on them, and message us your car model and year. We'll confirm the right sizes before you order.

      Do I need to go to a shop to install?

      No. Lift the arm, slide the old blade off, click the new one on and lower the arm. It takes about a minute per blade with no tools.
